1. Frontend and backend are deployed as separate ClusterIP services. To access them locally:

   Frontend:
   kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "ecommerce-app.fullname" . }}-frontend 8080:{{ .Values.frontend.service.port }}
   Then visit http://127.0.0.1:8080

   Backend:
   kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "ecommerce-app.fullname" . }}-backend 8081:{{ .Values.backend.service.port }}
   Then visit http://127.0.0.1:8081
